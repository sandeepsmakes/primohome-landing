Service conditions

  • PrimoQuick: Customer-selected priority tasks only; up to 1 hour (1–2 BHK) or 2 hours (3–4 BHK).
  • PrimoFresh: Standard cleaning of safely accessible areas; no detailed deep-cleaning tasks.
  • PrimoMax: All PrimoFresh tasks, plus the indicated detailed deep-cleaning tasks.
  • PrimoExtra: Separately quoted add-ons, subject to available equipment and agreed scope.
  • All services: PrimoHome brings agreed materials, supervisor checks the work, and customers inspect before payment.
  • Work requiring heavy furniture movement, dismantling or hazardous cleaning is not included.
